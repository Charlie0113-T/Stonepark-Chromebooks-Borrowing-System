# Stonepark Chromebook Manager — A Quick Guide for Staff

**[stonepark-chromebook-manager.vercel.app](https://stonepark-chromebook-manager.vercel.app)**

Everything below fits on three pages. You will probably only need the first two sections.

---

## 1. Booking a Chromebook or cabinet

1. Open the site and sign in
2. On the **Dashboard**, find the cabinet or Chromebook you want
3. Press **Book**
4. Check the details and press **Confirm Booking**

Your name is filled in for you. Change it if you are booking on someone else's behalf.

**The colours tell you what's free:**

| | |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **Available** | Nothing booked — go ahead |
| 🟡 **Partial** | Some devices are out, some are free |
| 🔴 **Full** | Everything is booked for right now |

A red cabinet is not necessarily unavailable *later*. Press **Book**, pick your
time, and the form will tell you how many are free **for that time slot**.

---

## 2. Returning them

**Scan the QR code on the cabinet with your phone camera.** Tap the link, press
**Return All**, done.

The first time, it asks you to sign in. After that it remembers you for 30 days,
so returning is two taps.

No phone handy? Open the site → **Bookings** tab → find your booking → **Return**.

---

## 3. Signing in without typing a password

Two things make this less annoying:

**Let your browser save the password.** When you sign in, your browser will offer
to save it. Say yes. It fills in automatically after that.

**Or set up a passkey** — sign in with your fingerprint, face, or the code you
use to unlock your phone. No password at all.

To set one up: sign in normally, press **🔑 Passkeys** at the top of the page,
then **Add a passkey on this device**.

Worth knowing:

- Add one on each device you use — your phone *and* your laptop
- Don't add one on a shared classroom device; it ties to your personal account
- Your password still works, always. A passkey is a shortcut, not a replacement

---

## 4. If something goes wrong

**"Could not reach the server… it may still be waking up"**
The server sleeps when nobody has used it for a while. Wait up to a minute — it
retries by itself. Nothing is broken and nothing is lost.

**It asks me to sign in again**
Sessions last 30 days. Just sign in again. If you set up a passkey, it's one tap.

**I forgot my password**
Press **Forgot password?** and answer your three security questions (favourite
food, book, colour). Spelling and spaces matter; capital letters don't.

Never set those up? Ask an admin to reset your password.

**"This email is not on the whitelist"**
Your address hasn't been added yet. Press **Sign Up** and then **Apply for
Access** — an admin will approve you.

**I booked the wrong thing**
**Bookings** tab → find it → **Cancel**. If devices were already collected, use
**Return** instead.

---

## 5. Two things that catch people out

**Booking ≠ collecting.** Booking reserves the devices; you still walk to the
cabinet and take them. Nothing unlocks automatically.

**Please return them in the system, not just physically.** Putting the
Chromebooks back on the shelf doesn't tell anyone. Until you press Return, the
cabinet shows as busy and your colleagues will think it's unavailable. That's
what the QR code on the cabinet is for — it takes about five seconds.

---

## Still stuck?

Ask whoever administers the system at Stonepark. Admins can reset passwords, add
staff, and fix bookings. Nothing in this system is permanently breakable — a
wrong booking can always be cancelled or returned.
